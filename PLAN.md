# FIRST FIVE WEEKS

Background reasoning per topic lives in MAIN.md. This file is the program: what starts when, what the ceiling is, and what gets ticked. The tick sheet is LOG.md.

Two design rules shape everything below:

1. **One new thing per week, at most.**
2. **Every addition is gated.** A daily item "held" if it was done on at least 5 of 7 days. If any required item is below 5/7 at the Sunday review, the next week repeats the current week. Repeating is the plan working, not the plan failing.

Habits take around two months to become automatic, not three weeks. The awake part gets cheaper each week because the environment does more of the work. Judge nothing before week 5 except whether the structure is holding.

## Standing rules

These sit above every week.

- **Never miss twice.** A miss costs nothing and triggers nothing. No make-up sessions, no doubling tomorrow.
- **Social evenings override the evening chain.** Mark the day with `-`. The morning anchor holds the next day regardless.
- **Sunday review, one change maximum.** Up or down. One.
- **Ceilings bind.** Feeling great in week 2 is when the ceiling does its job. It is not a signal to add.
- **Bed when sleepy, not before.** The bedtime target is an "in bed by", not a "stay up until".
- **Father's things go in one box**, closed, revisited in six months. Not a task, a rule.

## The two anchors

Program a day, not a list of habits. Everything attaches to one of two moments. The floor is the smallest version that still counts, for bad days. Never zero.

| Anchor               | Floor                                                            | Target                                                      |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Morning, on waking   | phone stays where it charged, ride to the shop and buy one thing | coffee, ride, buy today's food, eggs, no phone for the hour |
| Evening, 22:00 alarm | phone on charger, screens off, kitchen lights off                | kitchen closed, reset, journal, book, in bed by target      |

Written as triggers:

- After I wake, I leave the phone where it charged and make coffee.
- After coffee, I ride to the shop and buy today's food.
- After the ride, I eat eggs.
- After I finish eating, I close the kitchen.
- When the 22:00 alarm sounds, I plug the phone in and switch every screen off.
- After I plug the phone in, I write one page. _(from week 2)_
- After the page, I reset the living room for ten minutes with the podcast on. _(from week 2)_
- After the reset, tea and the book. Bed when sleepy.

## Setup day

The weekend before week 1. One-time tasks, not habits. None of these go on the sheet.

- [ ] Alarm clock bought, set for 07:00 as backup
- [ ] Wind-down alarm set for 22:00 on the phone
- [ ] Charger moved out of the bedroom (hall, kitchen or living room)
- [ ] Phone to grayscale
- [ ] Every non-human notification off
- [ ] Short-video apps deleted
- [ ] Autoplay off on every streaming service
- [ ] Games sorted: finite stay, infinite uninstalled
- [ ] Real coffee in place, cans off the shopping list
- [ ] First batch cook: eggs boiled, chicken cooked for three dinners
- [ ] Fridge stocked for weekday breakfast (eggs, ham, tomato, cucumber) and quark
- [ ] Book on the bedside table
- [ ] Notebook and pen next to the charger, not in the bedroom
- [ ] Fitify set to beginner, one short no-jumping bodyweight strength routine chosen. Not started until week 3.
- [ ] Baseline weigh-in, recorded in LOG.md
- [ ] LOG.md week 1 dated

### Week 1: anchors only

Required: phone out at alarm, phone-free first hour, morning ride, kitchen closed, in bed by 23:45.

Deliberately thin. Everything else in MAIN.md is permitted but is not on the sheet, so it cannot be missed. The phone-free first hour comes almost free: the phone charged in another room and the ride fills the hour. The morning ride is also the morning light, so coffee outside is optional. Between the alarm and bed: tea, shower, book. Eat at maintenance.

Sunday: batch cook (eggs, chicken), fifteen-minute review, record screen time.

### Week 2: the evening chain

New: journal five minutes at the alarm (reflect on the day, tomorrow's list, and a place for grief), then a ten-minute reset of living room and kitchen with the podcast on, then book. Podcasts and audiobooks are now cleaning-only. Laundry: one load on Sunday during batch cook, folded and put away in the same session. Decide the episode count before the TV goes on.

The journal is not ticked. If the sheet got filled in, the journal happened.

This is the danger week. Novelty dies around day ten and nothing is automatic yet. Both additions cost fifteen minutes at an anchor that already exists. No upward change permitted.

Sunday: weigh-in (a fortnight from baseline).

### Week 3: strength enters

New: Fitify on Tuesday and Friday, the shortest no-jumping beginner bodyweight strength routine. End each session wanting more. Food log begins: write down what you eat, no target, no restriction. It is a baseline, and it is how under-eating gets caught as training starts.

Muscle soreness that fades in a day or two is expected. Knee or hip pain still present after two days means drop back a phase.

### Week 4: zones and the cleaner question

New: one cleaning zone per weekday, fifteen minutes with a timer, stop at the bell. Suggested mapping, edit to fit the flat:

- Mon: bathroom
- Tue: floors
- Wed: surfaces and dusting
- Thu: bedroom and wardrobe
- Fri: fridge and kitchen deep

Everything has a home. If it does not, it gets one or it leaves. Decide at the Sunday review whether a cleaner every two weeks takes the deep clean.

Sunday: weigh-in.

### Week 5: first extension

New: one easy weekend ride, up to thirty minutes, conversational pace. At the Sunday review, set a small deficit from the two weeks of food logs and the three weigh-ins. A few hundred calories below what the log shows. Not more.

Walks begin week 6 at the earliest, ten minutes, only if knees are quiet after two weeks of strength work. Not week 5, because week 5 already has the weekend ride.

## Daily ticks and weekly numbers

Tick during the journal slot at the alarm. `1` done, `0` not done, `-` social override.

Daily, from week 1:

- Phone out at alarm
- Phone-free first hour
- Morning ride done
- Kitchen closed
- In bed by target

Weekly:

- Screen time total (from week 1)
- Laundry put away (from week 2)
- Strength sessions count (from week 3)
- Weight, fortnightly

Nothing else. No minutes, pace, heart rate or weights lifted before week 8. Do not build a tracking tool before week 5. The sheet is LOG.md.

## Sunday review

Fifteen minutes, on batch-cook day.

1. Count the ticks. Any required daily item below 5/7 means next week repeats this week.
2. Record screen time. Record weight if it is a weigh-in week.
3. One change, maximum. Up or down.
4. Set next week's row in LOG.md.
5. Batch cook. Laundry (from week 2).

## After week 5

Weeks 6 to 8 follow the exercise plan in MAIN.md: daily ride, weekend ride, two to three Fitify sessions, walks from week 6 if knees are quiet. The deficit stays small. Review whether the tick sheet still earns its place, and only then consider building something to replace it.

Work and Friends are separate sessions and not yet in this program.
