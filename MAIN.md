## Dopamine

Dopamine is about anticipation and wanting, not about pleasure. It will take a few weeks to reverse the effects of the bad habits. The worst one is phone scrolling, followed by gambling style video games. Also be mindful of youtube, snacking, alcohol and porn.

Willpower is a depleting resource that is naturally caught by my father's passing. That's ok. Change the environment so the default is a good choice:

- no or time-limited reels
- phone to grayscale
- charge phone outside bedroom
- turn off notifications that aren't human

Replace with:

- podcast/audiobook
- real book
- visit friends

Install the rule: Consumption comes after effort, where effort is work, a walk, a cooked dinner. Decide the amounts of consumption before starting.

Protect first and last hour of the day. **No phone for the first hour of the day**. Period. No phone during the last hour before sleeping.

Reintroduce low-stimulation time on purpose. Drink coffee without a screen, walk/bike without headphones. **Motivation regenerates in these gaps**.

Track weekly screen time. Just the number.

The first two weeks with be flat and irritable.

### Measurables

- weekly screen time
- when honored/slipping
- maybe initially log consumption reasoning

## Sleep

Already quite on track with the 7AM wakeup. Might be able to manufacture sleepiness.

- anchor clock by experiencing morning light right after wakeup
- coffee only before lunch
- stay away from alcohol
- dim living room lights in the evening as well, install smaller lamps
- warm shower before bed (meh)
- no late meals

**Install wind-down alarm to cut all screens**. One hour before planning to go to sleep. Decide the end of the evening before starting it, decides how many episodes to watch.

Build a ritual, rather setting than a fixed time. Clean kitchen + lights down + tea + book, naturally go to bed when sleepy.

Before starting the ritual, reflect on the day and lay out what to do tomorrow (we know this helps). Give grief a place here as well.

When still awake after 20 minutes, return to ritual. Bed is for sleep only.

Consider reading a book in bed to replace phone.

Winddown alarm at 22:00. Put phone to charge at that moment. Journal/reflect + plan. Then book.

Could play around with 22:00, depending on how this goes. Expect groggy few first days.

### Measurables

- winddown alarm honored
- bedtime

## Food

Already in a good place, but cut snacking by fixing the diet.

Low-carb better than keto, more easy to maintain. Rename "failed keto" to "low-carb".

Cut very processed foods (more important in the US).

Design 5 to 7 meals for rotation (see below). Protein at every meal.

Only 3 meals a day, clean kitchen afterwards.

Don't worry about social eating.

For the coming few weeks, focus on attaining structure and closing the kitchen daily.

## Exercise

Heart, lungs and muscles adapt in weeks, similar to younger people. **Tendons and ligaments DO NOT!!** They take months to adapt. Recovery time is longer for older people, so plans require fewer hard days.

Also, focus on biking rather than walking, until weight is lower.

First 4 to 6 weeks feel like nothing is happening (mostly neural and connective). That needs to be accepted.

Start with two short fitify sessions per week, focus on bodyweight strength routines.

Raise total volume by max. 10% each week. End every session wanting more. Don't buy extra gear.

General order to get going: ride bike, then strength, then weight loss, then walking

### Tentative Plan

- weeks 1 and 2: daily grocery ride
- weeks 3 and 4: daily grocery ride + 2 fitify sessions
- weeks 5 to 8: daily grocery ride + weekend ride + 2 to 3 fitify sessions + 3 10 minute walks
- weeks 9 to 12: daily grocery ride + long weekend ride + 2 to 3 fitify sessions + kettlebell + daily 10 minute walks

later: HIIT, daily fitify

### Measurables

Explicitly do not measure anything to remove obsession temptation
