## Points for Chris

1. Also for Ben: 3 years ago, we started as an actual startup, trying to begin a robotics business together, with everything that entailed. This is the most interesting challenge and also what attracted me to the project.

But, along the way, it turned into a department with engineers, where "everything else" disappeared beyond the horizon into a monthly report by Chris. I lost sight on what it is we're actually trying to do, about the business details.

What would be a good way to reinvigorate the startup part of this, regain back the insight, and figure out what we actually want with Mind Children? Ideally we recreate our vision, understanding that SNET is currently our biggest/only customer (quite literally), but try to achieve a longer distance goal with some clear high-level steps.

2. Also for Ben: I've been developing Thalamus for the past 3 months.

Thalamus is a full A/V pipeline for social robotics with 3D face pose and eye gaze detection, face and voice ID, support for directional microphones and depth camera, audio and visual speaking detection, multilingual speech recognition and generation, 3D people tracking around the robot, multiperson interaction agent using LLM or OmegaClaw, and lipsync and gesture generation. All aggressively optimized for Jetson Orin, ROS2 wrapper for Codey, and a bunch of more features. This doesn't really exist yet (the closest is a project from NVidia that connects STT-LLM-TTS).

I want to publish this as open source research and create a youtube channel where I take on the engineering challenges for this.

- This can be used to actually research social robotics.

- It is a way to advertise OmegaClaw and what OmegaClaw can do for social robotics.

- And it is also a way to put Mind Children out there for other institutes and robotics companies to notice.
