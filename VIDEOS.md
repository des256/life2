## LLM AI isn't going to work

- creativity is the most important human quality, the outlier choices are what make humans valuable
- LLMs are fundamentally not creative, they cannot produce outlier choices; paperclip/Indian example, storytelling
- frontier model scam: huge datacenter needs are unsustainable, currently running mostly on investors, Pascal's Wager "logic"
- jobs will change of course, but not disappear

The key to success is where YOU excel, where YOU find play, where YOU see things that others can't, what other people find boring; develop expertise away from the mean, and use LLMs to automate the boring stuff
